My friends

I’ve made friends in class, on the golf course and in the flute section. It’s fun when someone I know from one place turns up in another.

It’s easy to spend a whole week on homework and practice. I like making time to sit with someone and hear what’s going on in their life. Sometimes a friend wants advice. Sometimes they just want company.

Getting the band together

When our school music festival was coming up, I asked some classmates from my IGCSE Music course if they’d like to form a band. We chose Viva la Vida.

We played it in front of the whole school. I got to share that stage with the people I usually sit next to in class, all because we’d decided to try a song together.
