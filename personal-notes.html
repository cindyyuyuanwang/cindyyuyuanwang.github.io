A few personal notes

My friends say I’m the same person wherever they see me. They say I’m cheerful, easy to talk to, and good at making them laugh. That sounds like me.

I like being the person a friend can come to when something’s bothering them. And given the choice between an afternoon on my own and one spent with friends, I’d pick the friends almost every time.

At home

My mum says I’m usually the one who cheers everyone up at home. She likes that I make a point of asking our older relatives how they’re feeling. I’m glad she notices the little things.

Something I’d like to do next

I joined the school football team in Grade 7 and played in Shanghai’s interschool league. Lately, between homework, golf and flute, I haven’t been on the pitch much. I’d like to play again and try a sport I’ve never played before. It would be fun to be a beginner again.
